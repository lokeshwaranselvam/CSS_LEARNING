Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017)

Peter Parker tries to balance his life as a high school student and a superhero after joining the Marvel Cinematic Universe. Eager to prove himself to Tony Stark, he faces the dangerous Vulture, a villain who uses advanced alien technology. The film focuses on Peter's growth, responsibility, and desire to become a true hero.

Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019)

Following the events of Avengers: Endgame, Peter Parker travels across Europe on a school trip while grieving the loss of Tony Stark. His vacation is interrupted when he teams up with Mysterio to battle mysterious elemental creatures. Peter soon learns that things are not always as they seem and must step up to protect the world on his own.

Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

After Mysterio reveals Spider-Man's identity to the world, Peter's personal life falls apart. Seeking help from Doctor Strange, he accidentally opens the multiverse, bringing villains from other Spider-Man universes into his world. Peter faces his greatest challenge yet, learning difficult lessons about sacrifice, responsibility, and what it truly means to be Spider-Man.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day (Upcoming)

Spider-Man: Brand New Day is set to continue Peter Parker's journey after the events of No Way Home. With the world forgetting who he is, Peter begins a fresh chapter of his life, focusing on both his personal struggles and superhero responsibilities. The film is expected to explore a more independent Spider-Man as he rebuilds his life from the ground up.
